The essentials, considered

Tools that earn
a place in your day.

Made for practical everyday browsing: fewer distractions, useful controls, and your familiar workflow close at hand.

Focus mode

Hide the browser tabs and toolbar when you want the page itself to take center stage. Toggle it with Ctrl + Shift + F.

Quick links & bookmarks

Keep your most-used sites on the new tab page. Save a page with Ctrl + D and get back to it in a click.

Ad & tracker blocking

Block known ad and tracking domains, add your own blocked sites, and switch the built-in blocker on or off.

Search your way

Choose DuckDuckGo, Google, Brave Search, or Bing as your default search engine.

Keyboard-first controls

Open, close, and switch tabs; jump to the address bar; navigate back and forward; and adjust zoom with shortcuts.

Spotify mini player

Keep a compact Spotify player close by. Sign in, play links, and save tracks or playlists for quick access.

Your look, your accent

Choose a lime, mint, cyan, orange, or pink accent for the browser’s built-in pages.

Simple data controls

Clear cookies and cached files from Settings, or restore the default new-tab shortcuts when you need a reset.

One shortcut to quiet

When it’s time to focus, the browser can step back.

Focus mode hides VOID’s tabs and toolbar so the active page fills the window. Your keyboard shortcut brings the controls back when you need them.

More headroom for your work

Made for modest hardware, too.

VOID keeps its own interface restrained: a small set of browser controls, a simple new-tab page, and no extra dashboards to manage. It’s designed to feel at home on lower-end PCs as well as newer ones.

Web pages and active tabs still use their own memory and processing power. VOID does not set a guaranteed RAM or speed target.
